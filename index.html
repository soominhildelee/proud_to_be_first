Why this exists

First-generation students at Georgia Tech face challenges that nobody at home has been through before, and the support built for them is scattered across dozens of offices and web pages. Without one place to look, it is hard to find critical information or to meet the peers who understand.

Proud to be First brings those resources into one hub, so you spend less time searching and more time on the college experience you came for.

How to use it

  • Read the guidesStep-by-step guides on the academic journey, from career fairs to financial aid to coursework. Written by students and checked by GT staff.
  • Find people who get itRecommendations for where to get advice and mentorship from people who know what being first-gen is like.
  • Ask a questionEvery topic links straight to the official GT office that can help. A question form is on the way, and answers will turn into new guides.
